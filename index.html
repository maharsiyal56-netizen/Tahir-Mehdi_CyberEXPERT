About Me

Foundations first, offense next.

I'm a BSCS student with a strong pull toward how networks are built, defended, and — eventually — tested for weakness. I'm currently working through CCNA to make sure my grasp of routing, switching, and IP fundamentals is solid before going further into security.

Alongside coursework, I build and break things in Cisco Packet Tracer, GNS3, and virtual lab environments — simulating topologies, configuring devices, and practicing the troubleshooting instincts security work depends on.

The long-term goal: a career in penetration testing and network security, built on real hands-on skill rather than just theory.
